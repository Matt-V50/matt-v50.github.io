I'm a PhD student at [CVISS Lab](https://cviss.net/), University of Waterloo, advised by [Prof. Chul Min Yeum](https://www.linkedin.com/in/chul-min-yeum-29b60224a/).
I received my master's degree in Artificial Intelligence from the University of Chinese Academy of Sciences (UCAS) in 2024.
My research interests include 3D computer vision, Gaussian Splatting, computer graphics and 3D generative models.

During my master's, I was very fortunate to be advised by [Prof. Jianwei Guo](https://jianweiguo.net/) and [Prof. Xiaopeng Zhang](https://people.ucas.ac.cn/~zhangxiaopeng?language=en) at the [NLPR Lab](https://nlpr-web.ia.ac.cn/en/), Institute of Automation, Chinese Academy of Sciences (now merged into [MAIS Lab](https://mais.ia.ac.cn/)).

Before graduate school, I spent six years in industry as an algorithm engineer at [Tingyun](https://www.tingyun.com/), working on time-series forecasting and anomaly detection.
